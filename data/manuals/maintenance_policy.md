# Maintenance Policy (fictional training document)

## POL-01: Urgency levels
Level 1 WATCH: risk below the alarm line and estimated cycles left above 60. Keep routine checks and re-review at the next scheduled check.
Level 2 PLAN: risk at or above the alarm line, or estimated cycles left between 30 and 60. Schedule an inspection within the next 20 cycles and pre-order likely parts.
Level 3 URGENT: estimated cycles left between 10 and 30. Inspect at the next available stop and restrict to light duty.
Level 4 STOP: fewer than 10 cycles left, or risk above 95% with several sensors worsening. Take the machine out of service and inspect before any further use.

## POL-02: Before any inspection
Isolate power and apply lockout/tagout. Record the sensor readings and cycle count. Use only approved tools and log every finding in the maintenance record.

## POL-03: Several sensors drifting together
When three or more sensors drift together, treat it as general gas-path wear rather than a single faulty part. Raise the urgency by one level and inspect the whole path, not just one component.

## POL-04: Limits of predictions
Model risk and remaining-life figures are statistical estimates, not diagnoses. Confirm by physical inspection before replacing any part, and record the outcome so the models can be improved.
