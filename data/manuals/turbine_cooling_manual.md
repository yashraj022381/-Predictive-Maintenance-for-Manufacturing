# Turbine and Cooling Manual (fictional training document)

## TUR-01: LPT outlet temperature rising
Signature: low-pressure turbine outlet temperature (s4) above its healthy typical level.
Likely causes: turbine blade wear, loss of cooling air, or inefficient upstream compressors passing hotter air.
Actions: borescope-inspect turbine blades; check cooling flow (see COOL-01); review compressor findings (see HPC-01).
Escalate if: temperature rises while coolant bleed flow falls.

## COOL-01: Coolant bleed flow dropping
Signature: HPT coolant bleed (s20) or LPT coolant bleed (s21) below typical.
Likely causes: clogged cooling passages, leaking or stuck bleed valves, worn seals. Less cooling can overheat turbine blades.
Actions: inspect and flow-test bleed valves and lines; clear blocked passages; inspect turbine blades for heat damage.
Escalate if: coolant flow keeps falling or turbine outlet temperature (s4) rises.

## BLD-01: Bleed enthalpy rising
Signature: bleed enthalpy (s17) above its healthy typical level.
Likely causes: compressor inefficiency heating the bleed air, heat soak in the core.
Actions: verify against compressor temperatures (see HPC-01 and LPC-01); inspect bleed lines for damage.
Escalate if: other core temperatures are also rising.
